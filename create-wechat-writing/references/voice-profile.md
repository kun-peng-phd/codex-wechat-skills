# Ag Commodity Research: Chinese Voice Profile

Use this profile only when the user requests this house voice or supplies a matching past article. The August 2026 WASDE sample is the reference: precise, understated, readable, and built around how similar data shocks produce different balance-sheet outcomes.

## What to preserve

- Lead with a measured contrast: the report did not move all commodities in one direction; name the differences immediately.
- Each crop section begins with a clear judgment, then traces the arithmetic from yield, area, production, use, and stocks. Put the adjustment that actually explains the stock change ahead of decorative market color.
- Give a numerical statement its qualification nearby: U.S. versus global; official forecast versus actual harvest; inferred disappearance versus direct feed use; U.S. stock versus Chinese landed cost.
- Write in fluent industry Chinese. Terms such as “库存缓冲空间”“期初库存”“同装期报价”“压榨需求” are natural when they are the right economic terms. Spell out an unusual unit on first use.
- Vary paragraph length slightly; a short one-sentence judgment may sit beside a fuller explanatory paragraph. Avoid a mechanical identical template in every section.
- End with two to four specific observations that could confirm or revise the conclusion.

## Tone calibration

Prefer “库存缓冲空间扩大” to “库存突然多出一层垫子”; “供应预测上修，但当日价格未同步回落” to “价格没有照着走”; “新增供应被需求吸收” to “需求把供应吃掉”. These are examples, not forbidden-word rules.

Avoid inflated certainty, theatrical metaphors, English research jargon left untranslated, and formulaic transitions like “综上所述”. A modest “只看美国，这张表略有转松；全球并非同一个画面” is closer to the intended voice than a grand declaration of a universal market turn.

Do not copy the August article's sentences as a template. Reproduce its level of precision and causal pacing while letting the new report's evidence determine the structure.
